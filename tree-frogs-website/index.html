FAQ

Questions, answered.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Tree Frogs carries full general liability and workers' compensation insurance, and our climbers follow ANSI A300 and Z133 safety standards. We're happy to share certificates before any work begins.

Do you offer free estimates?

All estimates are free with no obligation. Most quotes are delivered within one business day after an on-site visit.

Do you handle emergency storm damage?

We respond to storm-related emergencies 24 hours a day across the Quad Cities and surrounding communities. Call our emergency line and a crew will be dispatched.

What areas do you serve?

Moline, Davenport, Bettendorf, Milan, Geneseo, Kewanee, Annawan, and surrounding Quad Cities and Western Illinois communities. If you're nearby and not listed, give us a call.

Do you clean up after the job?

Always. Full cleanup is included with every service: brush is chipped, logs are removed (or stacked on request), and lawns are blown off and raked clean.
