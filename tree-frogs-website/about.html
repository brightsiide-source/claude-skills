About us

A small crew of tree people who happen to run a business.

Tree Frogs was started in 2008 by a former municipal arborist who got tired of seeing trees butchered by guys with chainsaws and a truck. We grew slowly, hired carefully, and built a reputation for honest scopes, careful work, and clean job sites.

What we believe

Three things, every job.

Safety first, always

ANSI Z133 isn't a poster on the wall — it's the way every job is set up, briefed, and worked. Period.

Honest scopes

We don't upsell removals when a tree can be saved, and we don't sell pruning a tree doesn't need.

Leave it cleaner

Our cleanup standard is "you can't tell we were here, except the tree is healthier."

Credentials

Certified, insured, accountable.

Our crew leads carry ISA Certified Arborist credentials. We hold full general liability and workers' comp coverage and can provide certificates for any job.
