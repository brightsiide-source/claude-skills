How It Works

Selling Your House to Us, Step by Step.

Four steps. No commission, no closing costs, no contingencies. Close on a date you pick — typically within 30 days.

The Four Steps

  1. 1. Tell us about the property

    Call, text, or fill out a form on any page of this site. We need three pieces of information to start: the address, a rough sense of the condition, and your timeline. That's it. Most initial calls take under five minutes. We don't ask for a Social Security number, a credit check, or proof of ownership at this stage — those come later, at closing, handled by the title company.

  2. 2. We pull Arizona comps and assess condition

    Within 24 hours, we pull recent sold comparables for your specific Arizona submarket — not city-wide averages. An Arcadia bungalow doesn't trade like a Maryvale ranch; Scottsdale Old Town doesn't predict North Phoenix new construction. We weight comps by recency (last 90 days preferred), distance, square footage, and condition.

    Sometimes we walk the property in person; sometimes a thorough photo set from you is enough. We'll tell you which we need.

  3. 3. Written cash offer in 24 hours

    You get a written offer — not a verbal range, not "we'll get back to you." The offer document shows:

    • The comps we used
    • Our condition adjustment (and what it's based on)
    • Our margin (we explain this rather than hide it)
    • The number we'd wire to you at closing
    • The closing date window

    No pressure to decide on the call. Take it to family, an attorney, or your own agent. Many sellers do.

  4. 4. Close on the date you choose

    If you accept, we open escrow with a Maricopa County, Pima County, or other Arizona county title company. You pick the closing date — typically 14–30 days out, sometimes as fast as 7 days if you're up against a Trustee's Sale or relocation deadline.

    At closing, the title company handles paperwork. You sign. Funds wired to your bank that day. We pay all closing costs — the offer number is the number you receive.

Common Questions About the Process

  • Is the offer really no-obligation? Yes. We've had sellers take our offer to an agent and list instead. No hard feelings.
  • Do you charge any fees? No. The offer is what hits your bank account. We pay all closing costs.
  • What if the house is in really bad shape? That's our specialty. See as-is.
  • What if I'm in foreclosure? AZ is a non-judicial Trustee's Sale state — fast timeline. We can usually close before. See foreclosure.
  • What if I'm out of state? Whole process can be handled remotely. Documents via overnight mail or remote online notarization.
