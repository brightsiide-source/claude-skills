The Kinds of Properties We Buy in Arizona

Most cash-buyer landing pages say "any condition." We actually mean it. A non-exhaustive list of properties we've closed on in Arizona:

  • Fire-damaged houses. Partial fire, full fire, smoke damage that insurance didn't fully cover. We buy them as they sit.
  • Monsoon and hail damage. Arizona monsoons are brutal — roof damage, HVAC failures, water intrusion into stucco. We buy with or without insurance claims pending.
  • Foundation problems from caliche soil. Common across Maricopa and Pima counties — cracked block, slab settling, sticking doors. We factor it into the offer rather than demand repair credits at closing.
  • Pool and yard neglect. AZ properties with green-pool issues, broken pool equipment, dead landscape, irrigation failures from heat damage.
  • Vacant snowbird homes that sat empty for years after the owner died or stopped traveling. Surface looks OK; underlying systems often need everything.
  • Code enforcement situations — Phoenix, Tucson, Mesa code violations that turned into "I just want this off my plate" decisions.
  • Hoarding properties. No judgment. Leave everything. We do the cleanout.
  • HVAC and roof at end-of-life. Replacing a roof and an HVAC system in AZ together can run $30k+. Many sellers don't want to fund that before selling.
  • Mid-rehab abandons. Started a flip during the 2021 boom, ran out of money, half-finished kitchen, walls open. We take it from where you left off.

How As-Is Pricing Actually Works

Our offer is based on the property's after-repair value (ARV) — what it would sell for once renovated — minus our estimated rehab cost and a margin. So a distressed property in Tempe with $80k of needed repairs and a $400k ARV might net you something in the $230k–$260k range as a cash offer.

The condition doesn't change whether we'll buy it. The condition does change what we can pay — because we're the ones doing the rehab after closing.

We're transparent about this math. On the call, we'll show our ARV estimate, our rehab estimate, and how we got to the offer. You can disagree with our numbers and shop the offer around — many sellers do.

Honest Note: When MLS Beats As-Is Cash

If the issue is cosmetic — outdated kitchen, old carpet, dated bathrooms — you may be better off with a traditional listing, even with time and commission. Investors compete hard for "needs cosmetic updates" houses in Phoenix and Scottsdale and you'll see multiple offers.

If the issue is structural, environmental, or legal — that's our lane. MLS buyers' financing falls apart on those. We don't.
