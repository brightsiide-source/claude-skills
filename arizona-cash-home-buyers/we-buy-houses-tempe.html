Why Tempe Homeowners Sell to Us

Tempe is dominated by Arizona State University — 80,000+ students, plus faculty, staff, and the surrounding employer ecosystem (State Farm, KPMG, Carvana, ADP). The seller situations are distinctly student-and-faculty driven:

  • Investor exits from student-rental properties. Tempe has one of the most concentrated student-rental markets in the Valley. Many owners purchased single-family homes to convert to bedroom rentals; new short-term-rental and zoning regulations have soured the math.
  • ASU faculty & staff relocations. Sabbaticals, tenure denials, hospital placements, and inter-university moves drive turnover.
  • State Farm Tempe-campus transfers — the regional office moves people in and out.
  • Estate sales of properties owned by ASU retirees in established neighborhoods like Maple-Ash or near Tempe Town Lake.
  • Carvana and other tech-corridor moves.
  • Pre-foreclosure Trustee's Sale situations.
  • Out-of-state owners who bought condos during the 2020-2021 boom and now want to consolidate.
