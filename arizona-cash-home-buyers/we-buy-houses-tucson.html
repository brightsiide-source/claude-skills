Why Tucson Homeowners Sell to Us

Tucson is Arizona's second-largest market and has a distinct character from Phoenix — slower-growth, more university-driven, more retirement-oriented. The seller situations we see most:

  • University of Arizona faculty & staff relocations. U of A is the dominant employer. Sabbaticals, tenure denials, hospital placements — tight timelines that don't fit the MLS.
  • Davis-Monthan AFB military moves. PCS orders move people in and out on weeks of notice, not months. We close fast and handle remote transactions.
  • Retiree downsizing from larger Foothills homes to maintenance-free patio homes or assisted living. Often paid-off houses needing significant updates.
  • Out-of-state inheritances from parents who retired to Tucson in the 1990s-2000s. Adult children typically live elsewhere and want a fast remote close.
  • Pre-foreclosure in Pima County — Arizona's non-judicial Trustee's Sale process is fast (90-120 days). We close before the auction.
  • Distressed property — older Tucson homes face foundation issues, swamp cooler vs. AC retrofits, monsoon damage, plumbing in caliche soil.
  • Investor exits from the rental properties near U of A that became management headaches.
