Why Gilbert Homeowners Sell to Us

Gilbert grew from a small farming town to one of the fastest-growing cities in the country — quickly enough that the housing stock today ranges from preserved 1920s farmhouses in the Heritage District to brand-new south Gilbert subdivisions. Seller patterns we see most:

  • Family upsizing/downsizing. Gilbert is heavy on growing-family demographics. Owners moving up to larger homes or downsizing to townhomes without carrying two properties.
  • Master-planned community equity sellers. Power Ranch, Val Vista Lakes, Seville — properties that have appreciated significantly. Owners want to lock in the equity quickly.
  • Tech-corridor relocations tied to Banner, GoDaddy, Northrop Grumman, Lockheed Martin, and other Gilbert-area employers.
  • Heritage District estate sales — older homes around the original downtown often passing to adult children with significant deferred maintenance.
  • Pre-foreclosure situations in Maricopa County.
  • Investor exits from rental properties along Gilbert Road and the Higley corridor.
  • Out-of-state owners who bought during the 2020-2021 remote-work boom and now want to consolidate.
