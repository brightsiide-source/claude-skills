How Arizona Divorce Affects the House

Arizona is a community property state. With limited exceptions, assets and debts acquired during the marriage are equally owned by both spouses regardless of whose name is on the title or who paid the mortgage. The marital home is usually the largest single community asset.

There's typically a 60-day waiting period from petition filing before a divorce can be finalized in Arizona. How the house gets handled usually follows one of three paths:

  1. One spouse buys out the other and keeps the house. Usually requires a refinance to remove the other spouse from the mortgage, plus a cash settlement for their community-property share.
  2. Both spouses agree to sell and split the proceeds per the decree. Usually listed with an agent — 60-90 days.
  3. Cash sale to a third party for speed and certainty. Both parties sign at closing; proceeds are split per the decree; both walk away the same day.

We're typically Path 3 — the cash buyer. We've worked with sellers in every stage from "we just decided" to "the decree is final and we need this done."

Why a Cash Sale Helps During an AZ Divorce

  • Speed. A 14-30 day close means you're not paying a mortgage on a shared house for months of MLS listing.
  • No showings. Strangers walking through what's still your home during an already-difficult time. One walk-through. Done.
  • Clean number. One firm cash offer both parties can accept or reject. No "buyer wants $5k off after inspection" surprise to re-negotiate while you're already stressed.
  • Same-day cash-out. Both parties get their share at closing, wired by the title company per whatever community-property split the decree specifies.
  • Confidentiality. No MLS listing photos online. No yard sign. No neighbors asking questions.
  • Reduced friction between spouses. No fights about staging, listing price, accepting which offer, or who pays the inspection.

Honest Note About Price

If both spouses are getting along, have time, and the house is in good shape — a traditional MLS listing will probably net more. Our offer is most compelling when speed, certainty, privacy, or reduced conflict matter more than the last few thousand dollars.
