Primary Cities

These are the cities with dedicated landing pages and detailed local context — ZIPs, neighborhood breakdowns, and the seller situations we see most in each market.

Phoenix

ZIPs 85001–85099. Largest AZ market. Arcadia, Biltmore, Encanto, North Phoenix, Maryvale, Ahwatukee, Sunnyslope, downtown.

Tucson

ZIPs 85701–85775. Sam Hughes, Catalina Foothills, Marana, Oro Valley, Vail. U of A, Davis-Monthan AFB-driven.

Mesa

ZIPs 85201–85215. Las Sendas, Eastmark, Dobson Ranch, downtown Mesa. Snowbird-heavy, Boeing & ASU Polytechnic employers.

Scottsdale

ZIPs 85250–85268. Old Town, McCormick Ranch, DC Ranch, Troon, McDowell Mountain, Gainey Ranch. Affluent, slow-growth, tourism + Mayo.

Chandler

ZIPs 85224–85249. Ocotillo, Sun Lakes, Fulton Ranch, downtown Chandler. Intel-driven tech hub.

Gilbert

ZIPs 85233–85298. Heritage District, Power Ranch, Val Vista Lakes, Seville. Family-oriented, fast-grown master-planned communities.

Glendale

ZIPs 85301–85311. Historic downtown, Arrowhead Ranch, Westgate, Cardinals/State Farm Stadium area. Working-family, Honeywell, Luke AFB.

Tempe

ZIPs 85281–85289. Mill Avenue District, Maple-Ash, Tempe Town Lake area. ASU campus dominates; State Farm, Carvana corporate.
