Why Glendale Homeowners Sell to Us

Glendale is the Valley's sports and entertainment hub — State Farm Stadium, Westgate Entertainment District, Mullett Arena — plus a long-established middle-class housing base. Seller situations we see most:

  • Working-family financial stress. Glendale's median income runs below the Valley average; pre-foreclosure and equity-withdrawal situations are more common than in Scottsdale or Chandler.
  • Honeywell aerospace and Luke AFB relocations. Honeywell's Deer Valley campus and the proximity of Luke AFB drive employee transfers.
  • Banner Thunderbird-area moves — medical professionals coming in and out on tight timelines.
  • Estate sales from longtime residents in historic downtown Glendale — properties typically with 40+ years of deferred maintenance.
  • Investor exits from rental properties along Camelback Road and 67th Avenue corridors.
  • Pre-foreclosure Trustee's Sale situations — AZ's non-judicial process is fast.
  • Westgate-area short-term-rental exits from the Cardinals/concerts wave of buyers from 2018-2021.
