Common Questions

Frequently Asked

What states do you operate in?

We provide consulting across 45 licensing jurisdictions spanning 38 U.S. states. Our team has deep regulatory expertise in Colorado, California, Oregon, Michigan, Illinois, Massachusetts, and New York, with growing coverage in emerging markets.

How do you measure your 98% first-pass inspection rate?

We track inspection outcomes for every client who completes our full compliance program. Since 2022, 98% have passed their first regulatory inspection without citations requiring corrective action. This covers 150+ business engagements across cultivation, processing, and dispensary operations.

How long does a typical engagement last?

Most compliance programs run 4 to 12 weeks depending on scope. A single-location audit and training takes about 4 weeks. Multi-site workforce training typically runs 8 to 12 weeks. We also offer ongoing retainer relationships for continuous compliance monitoring.

What is included in the free consultation?

A focused 30-minute conversation where we assess your current compliance posture, identify the biggest knowledge gaps on your team, and outline which services would have the most immediate impact. You walk away with at least two actionable recommendations — whether or not you engage us.

Do you help with new license applications?

Yes. Our licensing support service guides you from application strategy through approval. We help with documentation preparation, municipal requirement analysis, and renewal planning. Our clients have a 94% first-submission approval rate across state and local licensing bodies.

Can you train our entire staff remotely?

Absolutely. We offer both on-site and remote training for teams of any size. Our remote programs use live instructor-led sessions — not pre-recorded videos — with role-specific breakout groups for cultivation, processing, dispensary, and management staff.
