Why Rancho Cordova Sellers Call Us

Rancho Cordova has one of the most diverse housing stocks in the Sacramento region — from postwar starter homes to brand-new master-planned construction. That mix means we close on a wide range of seller situations:

  • Multifamily and duplex owners exiting older properties off Folsom Boulevard or near Old Cordova that have become management headaches.
  • Distressed single-family homes in the 1950s neighborhoods around Mills Park and the original Cordova grid.
  • Mather-area sellers dealing with former military housing or older homes that need significant work.
  • Newer-build sellers in Anatolia, Sunridge, and the Cordova Hills area who need a fast, certain close — often due to job changes at the nearby tech and government employers.
  • Out-of-state owners who inherited a Rancho Cordova rental and want to liquidate without flying back for inspections and showings.
