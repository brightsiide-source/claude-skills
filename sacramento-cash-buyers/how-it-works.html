How It Works

Selling Your House to Us, Step by Step.

Four steps. No commission, no closing costs, no contingencies. Close on a date you pick — typically within 30 days.

The Four Steps

  1. 1

    Tell us about the property

    Call, text, or fill out a form. We need three pieces of information to start: the address, a rough sense of the condition, and your timeline. Most initial calls take under five minutes.

  2. 2

    We pull Sacramento comps and assess condition

    Within 24 hours, we pull recent sold comparables for your specific submarket. An East Sacramento craftsman doesn't trade like a North Natomas new build; Folsom doesn't predict Citrus Heights. We weight comps by recency (last 90 days), distance, size, and condition.

  3. 3

    Written cash offer in 24 hours

    You get a written offer document showing:

    • The comps we used
    • Our condition adjustment
    • Our margin (we explain it rather than hide it)
    • The number we'd wire to you at closing
    • The closing date window

    No pressure to decide on the call.

  4. 4

    Close on the date you choose

    If you accept, we open escrow with a Sacramento County (or Placer / Yolo / El Dorado) title company. You pick the closing date — typically 14–30 days out, sometimes as fast as 7 days. At closing, you sign. Funds wired to your bank that day.
