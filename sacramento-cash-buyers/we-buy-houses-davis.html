Why Davis Homeowners Sell to Us

Davis is dominated by UC Davis — 40,000 students, plus faculty, researchers, and the UC Davis Medical Center workforce. That university concentration creates distinctive seller patterns:

  • UC Davis faculty and staff relocations. Sabbaticals, tenure-track moves, retirements, and inter-UC transfers all drive Davis-area turnover on academic-calendar timelines.
  • UC Davis Health physician moves. Hospital placements and academic-medicine transfers between UC campuses.
  • Investor exits from student-rental properties. Davis has a concentrated student-rental market; many investors bought single-family homes to convert and now want out as regulations tighten.
  • Estate sales from longtime Davis residents — many North Davis homes have been in the same family for 30-50 years with significant deferred maintenance.
  • Bay Area outflows who bought Davis homes during the 2020-2021 remote-work boom and now want to consolidate.
  • Pre-foreclosure situations in Yolo County. California's non-judicial Trustee's Sale process is fast — we close before the auction.
  • Out-of-state inheritances from Davis retirees with adult children elsewhere.
