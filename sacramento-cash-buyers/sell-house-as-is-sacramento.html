The Kinds of Properties We Buy

A non-exhaustive list of properties we've closed on in the Sacramento area:

  • Fire-damaged houses. Partial fire, full fire, smoke damage. California's urban-wildfire risk profile means this is common. We buy as they sit.
  • Flood and water damage. Properties along the American River, Sacramento River, or in the Pocket-Greenhaven flood plain with water-table or actual flood-event damage.
  • Foundation issues. Sacramento's expansive clay soils cause settling, cracking, sticking doors. We factor it into the offer rather than demand repair credits at closing.
  • Pool neglect. Many older Sacramento homes have pools that haven't been maintained — green water, broken equipment, dead surrounding landscape.
  • Code enforcement situations. City of Sacramento, Sacramento County, or municipal code citations.
  • Hoarding properties. No judgment. Leave everything. We do the cleanout.
  • Long-vacant houses. Owner died, family moved, house sat for years. Mold, broken pipes, animal damage.
  • Mid-rehab houses. Started a flip during the 2020-2021 boom, ran out of money, half-finished kitchen, walls open.
  • Tenant-occupied disasters. Bad tenants, properties trashed by previous occupants, lease complications.
  • Earthquake retrofit needed. Older Sacramento homes that haven't been bolted, soft-story properties, cripple-wall issues — we factor into our offer.

How As-Is Pricing Actually Works

Our offer is based on the property's after-repair value (ARV) — what it would sell for once renovated — minus our estimated rehab cost and a margin. So a distressed property in East Sacramento with $100k of needed repairs and a $700k ARV might net you something in the $440k-$490k range as a cash offer.

The condition doesn't change whether we'll buy it. The condition does change what we can pay — because we're the ones doing the rehab after closing.

We're transparent about this math. On the call, we'll show our ARV estimate, our rehab estimate, and how we got to the offer.

Honest Note: When MLS Beats As-Is Cash

If the issue is cosmetic — outdated kitchen, old carpet, dated bathrooms — you may be better off with a traditional listing, even with the time and commission. Investors compete hard for "needs cosmetic updates" houses in East Sac, Land Park, and Curtis Park and you'll see multiple offers.

If the issue is structural, environmental, or legal — that's our lane. MLS buyers' financing falls apart on those. We don't.
