FAQ

Common Questions, Honest Answers.

The questions we get most often from Sacramento-area sellers, answered without sales-speak.

The Process

How fast can you buy my house in Sacramento?

As fast as 7 days from offer acceptance. Most closings happen 14–30 days out at a Sacramento County title company. You pick the date.

How do you calculate the offer?

We pull recent sold comps in your specific Sacramento-area neighborhood, weight them by recency, distance, size, and condition, and factor in estimated repair costs and our margin. The written offer shows the math.

Is the offer really no-obligation?

Yes. The offer is typically good for 7 days. Plenty of sellers take it to a local agent for a second opinion and list instead.

What's the catch?

Honest catch: our offer will generally be below what a fully-staged, fully-inspected MLS listing might fetch over 90 days. You're trading the price difference for speed, certainty, no commission, no closing costs.

Money

Are there any fees?

No. No commission, no closing costs, no inspection fees, no repair credits. The offer number is the number wired to your bank at closing.

What if I owe more than the house is worth?

If significantly underwater, a short sale negotiated with your lender is usually better than a cash sale. We'll tell you that on the first call.

Property Condition

Do I need to clean or make repairs?

No. We buy as-is. Leave behind anything you don't want.

What if my house is in really rough shape?

Rough shape is our specialty. Fire, flood, foundation, hoarding, vacant homes — we buy them.

Do you buy properties with tenants?

Yes. We buy tenant-occupied properties, even with difficult tenants.

Specific Situations

What if I'm in foreclosure?

California is a non-judicial foreclosure state — Trustee's Sale timelines run about 4 months. We can usually close before the auction. See foreclosure.

What if I inherited the house?

We work with personal representatives and out-of-state heirs. California probate is notoriously slow (9-18 months) but simplified procedures exist for smaller estates. See inherited-house page.

What if I'm going through a divorce?

California is community property, with a 6-month minimum waiting period from filing. We can sell the marital home with both spouses' agreement. See divorce sale page.

What if I'm out of state?

The entire transaction can be handled remotely via overnight mail or remote online notarization.

About Us

Who actually buys the house?

Sacramento Property Solutions is locally operated. We use our own funds. Some properties we rent, some we renovate and resell, some we hold.

Are you a real estate agent?

No. We're not realtors. We don't list properties on the MLS.

What areas of the Sacramento metro do you serve?

Sacramento, Elk Grove, Roseville, Folsom, Citrus Heights, Rancho Cordova, Davis, West Sacramento, plus surrounding Sacramento, Placer, Yolo, and El Dorado county communities. Full list on our service-areas page.
