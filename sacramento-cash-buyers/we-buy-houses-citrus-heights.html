Why Citrus Heights Sellers Call Us

Citrus Heights is one of our favorite neighborhoods to buy in — it's full of well-built 1960s and 1970s ranch homes, many owned by long-time residents who are ready for a change. We hear from sellers every week dealing with:

  • Aging-in-place owners downsizing or moving in with family — the home is paid off but needs 30 years of updates that they don't want to manage.
  • Adult children inheriting a parent's home in the Sayonara or Sungarden area, often after years of deferred maintenance.
  • Tired landlords exiting single-family rentals near Sunrise Mall or Greenback Lane that have become more trouble than they're worth.
  • Pre-foreclosure situations where time is short and a traditional listing isn't realistic.
  • Sellers with code violations from Citrus Heights code enforcement — unpermitted additions, fence issues, or property maintenance citations.

Older homes don't scare us. We have crews ready to renovate, and we factor that into a fair offer up front — no inspection drama, no last-minute price reductions.
