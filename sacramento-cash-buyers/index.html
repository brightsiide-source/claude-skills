Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can you actually close?

As fast as 7 days from offer acceptance. Standard closings happen in 14–30 days at a local Sacramento title company. You pick the date that fits your timeline.

What types of houses do you buy?

Single-family homes, condos, townhouses, multi-family (2–4 units), and mobile homes on land. Any condition — from move-in ready to fire-damaged or distressed.

How do you determine your offer?

We pull recent Sacramento-area sold comps, factor in your home's condition, estimate repair costs, and subtract a reasonable margin. We share the math so you know it's fair.

Are there really no fees or closing costs?

Correct. No agent commissions, no closing costs, no inspection fees, no repair credits. The offer number is the number wired to you at closing.

Do I need to clean or repair anything?

No. Take what you want, leave the rest — even trash, old furniture, or unwanted items. We handle all of it after closing.

Will you list my house or actually buy it?

We're direct buyers, not agents. We use our own funds (or vetted partner funds) to close. You sign with a buyer, not a listing agreement.

What if my house has tenants, liens, or back taxes?

Not a problem. We routinely buy properties with tenants in place, code violations, IRS liens, HOA arrears, or back property taxes. The title company sorts it all out at closing.
