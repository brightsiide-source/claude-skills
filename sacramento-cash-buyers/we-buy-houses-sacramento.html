Why Sacramento Homeowners Sell to Us

Sacramento proper is one of the most diverse housing markets in Northern California — 1920s craftsmans, mid-century ranches, midtown lofts, and brand-new infill in North Natomas. With that diversity comes a wide range of seller situations:

  • State government relocations. Sacramento is the state capital — thousands of state employees move in and out on tight transfer timelines. Bay Area outflows are also a constant.
  • UC Davis Health, Sutter, Kaiser, Dignity physician and nurse moves on health-system timelines.
  • East Sacramento and Land Park estate sales. Many of the 1920s-1940s homes are passing to adult children — properties with original kitchens, knob-and-tube wiring, and decades of deferred maintenance.
  • Pre-foreclosure situations in Sacramento County. California is a non-judicial foreclosure state — Trustee's Sale timelines run roughly 4 months. We close before the auction. See our foreclosure page.
  • Out-of-state inheritances. Many Sacramento retirees' adult children live in the Bay Area, LA, Portland, or out of state — they want a fast remote close.
  • Bay Area equity-takers who bought in Sacramento during the 2020-2021 telework wave and now want to consolidate elsewhere.
  • Tired landlords with single-family rentals in Tahoe Park, Oak Park, or the Pocket who want to exit.
  • Distressed properties — fire damage from urban wildfires, foundation issues from expansive clay soils, code citations from City of Sacramento, long-vacant houses.
