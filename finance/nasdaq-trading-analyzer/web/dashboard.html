How it works
Regime filter — never fades a strong trend, so it won't tell you to catch a crash.
Confluence score (0–100) — grades each setup on RSI, EMA, VWAP, MACD, Bollinger and support/resistance alignment.
Scalp-calibrated — stops and targets sized to your NQ point range, not vague percentages.
News & market-hours guards — mutes alerts around major events and outside regular hours.
Outcome tracking — every alert is followed to a win or loss, so the edge is measured, not assumed.
Uses a NASDAQ ETF as the live proxy feed; you execute on your own platform.
