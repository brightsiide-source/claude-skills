Straight answers.

The questions a careful trader actually asks.

Is this financial advice?+

No. It's an educational and informational tool. The signals are algorithmic and can be wrong. You make every decision and place every trade yourself.

How is it different from other signal services?+

Most are black boxes — "trust our alerts." We show the rules, the confluence score behind every call, and a public win/loss record for each strategy. Nothing is hidden, including the strategies that don't work yet.

What does it cost?+

Nothing during the beta. Paid tiers arrive only after strategies are validated with real data — you'll never be asked to pay for an unproven edge.

Does it place trades for me?+

No. It watches the market and flags high-quality setups with an exact entry, stop, target and size. You execute on your own platform.

Which markets work today?+

NASDAQ futures (NQ/MNQ) are live now via a real-time proxy feed. Options, stocks and crypto are on the roadmap — pick your market in the app and you're on the early list.
