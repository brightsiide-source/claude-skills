About Hatch

A proud farming town with a world-famous reputation.

The Chile Capital of the World

Hatch is a small village of about 1,600 residents located in the fertile Rio Grande valley approximately 40 miles north of Las Cruces. Known globally as the "Chile Capital of the World," Hatch and the surrounding Hatch Valley produce some of the most sought-after green and red chile in existence. The annual Hatch Chile Festival draws thousands of visitors every Labor Day weekend.

The town's identity is inseparable from agriculture. Fields of chile, onions, pecans, and other crops stretch in every direction. Most homes in the 87937 ZIP code reflect the community's working-class, agricultural roots — you'll find older adobe and block construction, modest ranch-style homes, farmhouses with outbuildings and barns, and properties on irrigated agricultural land.

Hatch's small size and rural location mean that the traditional real estate market here moves slowly. Listing a home through an agent can take months or longer, especially if the property needs work or sits on agricultural land. We offer a faster, simpler alternative.
