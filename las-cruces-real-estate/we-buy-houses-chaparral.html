About Chaparral

An affordable, growing community at the southern edge of Dona Ana County.

Affordable Living Near the Border

Chaparral is a census-designated place of approximately 15,000 residents in the far southern portion of Dona Ana County, sitting along the New Mexico-Texas border east of Sunland Park. The community has grown significantly over the past two decades, attracting families seeking affordable housing within commuting distance of El Paso.

The 88081 ZIP code covers a sprawling area of desert terrain dotted with a mix of manufactured homes, modular homes, and site-built houses. Many properties sit on larger lots — half-acre or full-acre parcels are common. The community's unincorporated status means development has been organic, with properties varying widely in age, style, and condition.

Chaparral's affordability is its greatest strength, but it also means that some properties have challenges that make conventional selling difficult: manufactured homes on owned land, well and septic systems, unpaved roads, and deferred maintenance. We specialize in buying exactly these types of properties.
