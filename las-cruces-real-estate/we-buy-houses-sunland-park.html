About Sunland Park

A border city experiencing rapid growth and development.

A Gateway Between Two States

Sunland Park is a city of approximately 18,000 residents in the southernmost tip of Dona Ana County, sitting right along the New Mexico-Texas border. Its proximity to El Paso — just 15 minutes from downtown — gives residents access to big-city amenities while maintaining a smaller-town cost of living.

The area has experienced significant growth in recent years, driven by the Sunland Park Racetrack & Casino, the Santa Teresa industrial port of entry, and new residential developments catering to El Paso commuters. The 88063 ZIP code includes everything from affordable starter homes and mobile homes to newer subdivision construction.

Sunland Park's border location creates unique real estate dynamics. Some properties may have title complications, deferred maintenance from absentee owners, or other challenges that make traditional selling difficult. That's where we come in — we buy houses in any situation.
