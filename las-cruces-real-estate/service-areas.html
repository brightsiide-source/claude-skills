Where We Buy: The Mesilla Valley

Las Cruces, the surrounding Dona Ana County communities, and the southern New Mexico stretch from Hatch down to the Texas state line. Locally-owned, locally-operated.

Primary Cities

Cities with dedicated landing pages and detailed local context — neighborhoods, ZIP codes, and the seller situations we see most in each market.

Las Cruces

The Mesilla Valley's anchor city — about 110,000 residents. NMSU, White Sands Test Facility, Memorial Medical Center, plus a deep mix of housing from historic adobes to newer East Mesa builds.

Mesilla

Historic town just west of Las Cruces. Old Mesilla's plaza is a National Historic Landmark; surrounding housing stock ranges from preserved adobes to modern infill.

Sunland Park

Southern Dona Ana County, right at the Texas / Mexico borders. Distinct from the rest of the valley — close-knit, working-family community.

Anthony

Sits across the NM/TX state line. Agricultural roots, mid-valley location, mix of older and newer homes.

Dona Ana

Small community north of Las Cruces — older housing stock with significant generational ownership.

Chaparral

Census-designated place in southern Dona Ana County. Working-family demographics, often distressed-property situations.

Hatch

The chile capital of the world. Small agricultural community in northern Dona Ana County / southern Sierra County.
