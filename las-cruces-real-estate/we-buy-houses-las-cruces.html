The Heart of Southern New Mexico

Las Cruces is the second-largest city in New Mexico with over 111,000 residents and one of the fastest-growing metro areas in the state. Anchored by New Mexico State University, White Sands Missile Range, and a thriving agricultural sector, the city offers a unique blend of college-town energy, desert beauty, and small-city affordability.

From the established neighborhoods of Alameda and the Elks Club area to the newer developments in Sonoma Ranch and East Mesa, Las Cruces has a diverse housing stock. ZIP codes 88001 through 88012 cover everything from historic adobe homes near Downtown to modern subdivisions off Roadrunner Parkway.

Whether your property is a 1960s ranch-style home near Telshor Boulevard, a student rental close to NMSU, or a custom home in Picacho Hills — we make fair cash offers regardless of condition, age, or location.
