How We Got Started

Las Cruces Cash Home Buyers was founded in 2018 with a simple idea: homeowners deserve a better way to sell. We'd seen too many families struggle with the traditional real estate process — the months of waiting, the expensive repairs, the uncertainty of buyer financing falling through.

We started right here in Las Cruces because this is our home. We grew up in Dona Ana County, we raise our families here, and we're invested in this community. When we buy a house, we're not just closing a deal — we're helping a neighbor move forward with their life.

Since 2018, we've helped over 500 homeowners across Las Cruces, Mesilla, Sunland Park, Anthony, and every corner of Dona Ana County. From families facing foreclosure to landlords ready to retire, we've seen every situation — and we treat every homeowner with the respect and care they deserve.
