About Dona Ana

The namesake of the county — a community with deep New Mexico roots.

Quiet Country Living Minutes from the City

The community of Dona Ana sits along the Rio Grande just a few miles north of Las Cruces. As an unincorporated census-designated place, it retains a distinctly rural feel that sets it apart from the nearby city. The area is home to approximately 5,000 residents who value the open space, slower pace, and agricultural landscape.

Properties in Dona Ana (ZIP 88032) vary widely. You'll find older adobe and block homes on generous lots, small ranchettes with horse facilities, family compounds that have expanded over decades, and modest homes on agricultural parcels along the river valley. Many of these properties have been in families for generations.

The unincorporated status means some properties have unique characteristics — unpermitted additions, informal lot splits, shared driveways, or mixed-use agricultural and residential use. These factors can complicate traditional sales, but we buy properties with these complexities every day.
