Your Timeline

What to Expect, Day by Day

Here's a typical timeline when you sell your Las Cruces house to us. Every situation is unique, but this gives you a clear picture of how fast things move.

📅

Day 1: First Contact

You reach out via phone, form, or text. We ask a few questions about your property and schedule a convenient time to visit — often the same day or next day.

🔎

Day 1-2: Property Walkthrough

We visit your home for a quick, friendly walkthrough. This typically takes 15-30 minutes. No need to clean or prepare — we've seen it all.

💰

Day 2-3: Cash Offer Presented

Within 24 hours of our visit, we present a written cash offer with a full breakdown. Review it on your own time — no pressure, no expiration.

✍

Day 3-5: Agreement Signed

Once you accept, we open escrow with a local title company. They begin the title search and prepare closing documents while you start packing (or don't — we handle cleanout too).

📋

Day 5-7: Title Cleared

The title company verifies clear title. If there are any liens or issues, we work with the title company to resolve them — often from the sale proceeds.

🎉

Day 7+: Closing Day

You sign the final documents at the title company (or via mobile notary). Cash is wired to your account or handed to you as a cashier's check. Done.
