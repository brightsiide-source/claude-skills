About Anthony

A quiet border town with deep agricultural roots.

A Hardworking Agricultural Community

Anthony is a small town of about 9,000 residents that sits directly on the New Mexico-Texas border along the Rio Grande. The town is split between two states — Anthony, NM (ZIP 88021) and Anthony, TX — creating a unique cross-border community united by shared schools, churches, and a strong agricultural heritage.

The local economy revolves around farming and ranching, with pecan orchards, chile fields, and dairy operations dotting the landscape. Many homes in Anthony are modest, family-owned properties that have been passed down through generations. You'll find everything from older adobe and block homes along the main corridors to rural properties on larger lots with outbuildings and irrigation access.

Anthony's rural character means that properties here sometimes fall outside the comfort zone of traditional real estate agents. Homes on well water, with septic systems, or on agricultural land can be harder to sell through conventional channels. We buy these properties without hesitation.
