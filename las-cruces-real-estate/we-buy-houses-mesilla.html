About Mesilla

A historic gem just minutes from Las Cruces.

Where History Meets Community

Mesilla (also known as Old Mesilla) is a small, unincorporated community steeped in over 400 years of history. The Old Mesilla Plaza is the cultural heart of the area, drawing visitors from around the world to its galleries, restaurants, and the famous Basilica of San Albino. Billy the Kid once stood trial in the old Mesilla courthouse.

The town's character is defined by its thick-walled adobe homes, tree-lined streets, and a pace of life that feels a world apart from nearby Las Cruces. Mesilla Park, the adjacent residential area along Avenida de Mesilla, offers a mix of mid-century homes, newer construction, and properties that blend old-world style with modern living.

Properties in the 88046 and 88047 ZIP codes range from historic adobes that may need significant restoration to well-maintained family homes. We buy them all — regardless of condition, age, or whether they come with historic preservation considerations.
