Sell Your Lewiston Home Fast for Cash

Lewiston is the seat of Nez Perce County and one of Idaho's most historically significant cities. With a population of about 34,000, the LC Valley (Lewiston-Clarkston) area straddles the Idaho-Washington border at the confluence of the Snake and Clearwater rivers. The city's economy is driven by agriculture, Clearwater Paper Corporation, Lewis-Clark State College, and the Port of Lewiston — making it the most inland seaport in the western United States.

Lewiston's housing stock tells the story of a working river town. The Normal Hill neighborhood features stately homes from the early 1900s, many with panoramic views of the valley but needing updated systems and structural maintenance. The Orchards area has a mix of mid-century homes and newer development, while Downtown is home to historic commercial buildings and adjacent residential properties. Many of these older homes carry deferred maintenance that can make a traditional sale difficult — buyers want move-in-ready, and sellers can't always afford $30,000-$50,000 in pre-sale improvements.

Idaho Home Buyers provides an alternative. We purchase Lewiston homes as-is for cash, handling all closing costs and paperwork. Our team understands the LC Valley market — the influence of the paper mill, the college, and the agricultural economy on local property values. We price our offers based on Nez Perce County data, not statewide trends.
