A note on wells and septic in Eagle

A large share of Eagle's acreage and rural-feeling properties run on private wells and septic systems rather than city water and sewer. In a conventional sale, that often triggers well-flow tests, septic inspections, and repair demands that can stall a closing for weeks. We buy well-and-septic homes in Eagle as-is and take on that due diligence ourselves, so it never becomes a delay or a bill on your side of the table.

Ada County Selling Notes

A few Idaho-specific facts work in your favor when you sell in Eagle:

  • No real estate transfer tax. Idaho has no state or county transfer tax, so that cost simply doesn't apply to your sale.
  • Property taxes are paid in arrears. Ada County bills for the prior year, and taxes are prorated at closing — we handle that proration as part of a clean cash sale.
  • Homeowner's exemption. If your Eagle property is your primary residence, Idaho's homeowner's exemption may have lowered its taxable value, and that status gets reconciled at closing.
