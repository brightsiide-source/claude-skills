The Boise & Ada County Market, in Plain Terms

Boise's housing stock is unusually varied for a city its size. The North End and East End are full of early-1900s Craftsman and bungalow homes, many with the original systems still in place. The Bench holds mid-century ranches from the 1950s and 60s. West Boise and the areas along Eagle Road are dominated by newer subdivisions, while Southeast Boise, Barber Valley, and Harris Ranch mix higher-end newer construction with foothills lots. Each of these submarkets prices differently, and a national iBuyer's algorithm rarely gets Boise right.

Because we live and work in the Treasure Valley, we price from local comparable sales rather than a one-size-fits-all model. One detail that surprises many sellers: Idaho is a non-disclosure state, meaning final sale prices are not part of the public record the way they are in most states. That makes accurate local valuation harder for out-of-area buyers and is a big reason a genuinely local offer tends to beat a remote one.

How We Determine Your Boise Cash Offer

A note on wells and septic in the county

Many homes in unincorporated Ada County — including parts of Hidden Springs, Barber Valley, and the foothills — run on private wells and septic systems. In a traditional sale these often trigger inspections, certifications, and repair demands that stall closing. We buy well-and-septic homes as-is and handle that due diligence ourselves, so it never becomes your problem or your bill.

Ada County Selling Notes

A few Idaho-specific facts that work in your favor when you sell:

  • No real estate transfer tax. Idaho does not charge a state or county transfer tax, so that entire line item simply doesn't exist on your sale.
  • Property taxes are paid in arrears. Ada County taxes are billed for the prior year, so the closing agent prorates them — we handle that split as part of a clean cash close.
  • Homeowner's exemption. If the Boise home is your primary residence, Idaho's homeowner's exemption may have reduced your taxable value; that status is squared up at closing.
