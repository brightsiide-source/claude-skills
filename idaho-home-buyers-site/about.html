About Idaho Home Buyers

A local Idaho company on a mission to make selling your home simple, fast, and fair. No gimmicks, no runarounds — just honest cash offers from people who live and work in your community.

Our Story

Idaho Home Buyers was founded in 2018 with a straightforward idea: homeowners deserve a better option when they need to sell quickly. Too many Idaho families were trapped in a broken system — spending months on the market, paying thousands in agent commissions, making expensive repairs they could not afford, and still facing uncertainty about whether the deal would close.

We set out to change that. As born-and-raised Idahoans, we understood the unique challenges homeowners face across the Gem State. Whether it is a family navigating a difficult inheritance in Boise, a couple going through a divorce in Meridian, or a landlord dealing with a problem property in Twin Falls, we saw the same story repeated: good people stuck in stressful situations with no clear path forward.

Since our founding, we have helped hundreds of Idaho homeowners close on their terms. We buy houses in any condition — from move-in ready homes to properties that need significant work. Every offer we make is backed by our own cash, so there are never any financing contingencies or last-minute surprises. We cover all closing costs and handle the paperwork so our clients can focus on what comes next.

Today, we serve communities across Idaho, from the Treasure Valley to the Magic Valley, eastern Idaho, and the Panhandle. Our team lives here, raises families here, and reinvests in the neighborhoods where we operate. When you work with Idaho Home Buyers, you are not dealing with an out-of-state corporation — you are working with your neighbors.

Our Core Values

Everything we do is guided by four principles that shape how we treat every homeowner we work with.

♥

Integrity

We do what we say we will do. Every offer we make is the offer we honor at closing. No bait-and-switch tactics, no last-minute price reductions, and no hidden fees. Our reputation in the Idaho community is built on trust, and we protect it with every transaction.

⚡

Speed

We understand that when you need to sell, time matters. That is why we deliver cash offers within 24 hours and can close in as few as 30 days. Our streamlined process eliminates the weeks of waiting, showings, and uncertainty that come with a traditional sale.

⚖

Fairness

Our offers are based on real market data and transparent calculations. We walk you through exactly how we arrived at our number so you can make an informed decision. We never pressure anyone to accept, and we are always happy to answer questions.

☼

Transparency

No fine print, no surprises, no hidden agendas. From the first phone call to the closing table, you will always know exactly where things stand. We explain every step of the process and make sure you feel comfortable and informed before making any decisions.
