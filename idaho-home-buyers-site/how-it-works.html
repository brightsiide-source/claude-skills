Sell Your Idaho House in 3 Easy Steps

No realtors, no repairs, no waiting. Our streamlined process gets you a fair cash offer in 24 hours and puts money in your pocket in as few as 30 days.

Our Simple 3-Step Process

1

Submit Your Property Details

Getting started is easy and takes less than two minutes. Simply fill out our short online form or give us a call. We just need the basics: your property address, a general idea of its condition, and your preferred timeline for selling. There is absolutely no obligation and no pressure. We will never share your information with third parties or bombard you with calls. Every inquiry is handled personally by a member of our local Idaho team, not a call center. Whether your house needs major repairs, is behind on payments, or is in perfect condition, we want to hear from you.

2

Receive Your Fair Cash Offer

Within 24 hours of receiving your property details, we will present you with a transparent, no-obligation cash offer. Our offers are based on current Idaho real estate market data, comparable recent sales in your neighborhood, and the condition of your property. We take the time to explain exactly how we calculated our offer so you understand every dollar. There are no hidden fees, no deductions at closing, and no last-minute price reductions. If our offer works for you, great. If not, there is absolutely no hard feelings. We want every homeowner to feel confident and informed throughout the process.

3

Close and Get Paid

Once you accept our offer, you choose the closing date that works best for your schedule. Need to close in 30 days? No problem. Need a few months to find your next home? We can wait. We work with a trusted local Idaho title company to handle all the paperwork, and we cover 100% of the closing costs. On closing day, you receive your cash payment directly. There are no commissions to pay, no last-minute surprises, and no deductions. You walk away with exactly the amount we agreed on. Many of our clients use this process to avoid foreclosure, settle an estate, relocate quickly, or simply skip the months of uncertainty that come with a traditional listing.
