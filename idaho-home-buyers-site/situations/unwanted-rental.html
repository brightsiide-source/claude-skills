Exit the Landlord Business the Easy Way

Owning rental property in Idaho looked a lot more appealing before the 2 a.m. plumbing calls, the turnover costs, and the months chasing late rent. Whether you've inherited a rental you never wanted, you're done managing a property from out of state, or you're simply ready to cash out of an investment that's become more headache than income, we make the exit simple. We buy rental and investment properties across the Treasure Valley and the rest of Idaho for cash — and unlike a traditional sale, you don't have to empty the unit, fix it up, or wait for the right buyer.

The biggest obstacle most landlords face when selling is the tenant. List on the open market and you're often forced to either wait out the lease, coordinate showings around renters who'd rather not cooperate, or pursue an eviction to deliver a vacant home. We remove that problem completely. We buy occupied rentals and take over the existing lease at closing, so your tenants stay put and the transition is invisible to them. If you have a difficult tenant or unpaid rent, that becomes our responsibility the day we close.
