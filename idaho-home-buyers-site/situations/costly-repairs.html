We Buy Idaho Homes in Any Condition

When a house needs serious work, the traditional sale process turns against you. Agents push for repairs and updates before listing, buyers' inspectors flag every issue, and lenders often won't finance a home with major problems — meaning even a willing buyer can't close. Homeowners get stuck pouring money into a property they're trying to leave, or watching offers fall through after weeks of waiting. We remove that trap entirely. We buy houses exactly as they are, with cash, so the condition of your home is our problem to solve, not yours.

Idaho's housing stock includes plenty of older homes — 1950s ranches on the Boise Bench, aging farmhouses around Canyon County, and properties in Pocatello, Twin Falls, and Idaho Falls that have deferred maintenance for years. We've bought homes with collapsing roofs, foundation settlement, fire and water damage, dated electrical and plumbing, hoarder conditions, and unpermitted additions. There is no condition too rough for us to make a fair offer, and you never have to clean, repair, or even empty the house.

Problems We Buy Around Every Day

  • Foundation settlement, cracks, or structural issues
  • Roof damage, leaks, and aging or failed systems (HVAC, electrical, plumbing)
  • Water damage, mold, and flood or fire damage
  • Code violations, unpermitted work, and failed inspections
  • Outdated kitchens and bathrooms that would need full remodels to list
  • Hoarder homes, heavy clutter, and properties that need a full cleanout

Skip the Repair Bills and the Risk

Major repairs are expensive and unpredictable. A foundation fix can run tens of thousands of dollars, and once you open up walls or dig into a roof, the costs often grow. Even after spending the money, there's no guarantee a buyer's financing will hold together. Selling to us means you keep your cash, skip the contractors, and close with certainty. We cover all closing costs and charge no commissions, so the offer we make is what you walk away with.

An Honest As-Is Offer

We base our offer on what your home would be worth fully renovated, minus the realistic cost of the repairs and our holding costs — and we walk you through that math so nothing feels like a black box. Our team is local, so the comparable sales we use are real Idaho neighborhood figures, not a national estimate. You get a fair number, a fast close, and zero out-of-pocket cost.
