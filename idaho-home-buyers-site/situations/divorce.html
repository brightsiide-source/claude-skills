A Clean, Neutral Way to Divide the Home

When a marriage ends, the family home is often the largest shared asset — and the most complicated to untangle. A traditional listing can drag on for months, requiring both parties to agree on a price, coordinate repairs and showings, and keep the property presentable while emotions run high. Every week the house sits unsold is another week of shared mortgage payments, taxes, and friction. Selling directly to us for cash short-circuits all of that. We provide one fair offer, a firm closing date, and a clean lump sum that can be divided according to your settlement.

Idaho is a community property state, which generally means the home and other assets acquired during the marriage are owned equally between spouses. That makes a transparent, arms-length sale especially valuable: there's no question of one spouse handling the listing, choosing the agent, or negotiating repair credits in a way the other might dispute. We deal with both parties evenly and coordinate with your attorneys so the transaction stays neutral from start to finish.
