How It Works

Selling Your House to Us, Step by Step.

Four steps. No commission, no closing costs, no contingencies. Close on a date you pick — typically within 30 days.

The Four Steps

  1. 1. Tell us about the property

    Call, text, or fill out a form. We need three pieces of information to start: the address, a rough sense of the condition, and your timeline. Most initial calls take under five minutes. We don't ask for a Social Security number or credit check at this stage.

  2. 2. We pull Michiana comps and assess condition

    Within 24 hours, we pull recent sold comparables for your specific submarket — not city-wide averages. A Sunnymede colonial doesn't trade like a West Side ranch; downtown Mishawaka doesn't predict Granger. We weight comps by recency (last 90 days), distance, size, and condition.

  3. 3. Written cash offer in 24 hours

    You get a written offer document showing:

    • The comps we used
    • Our condition adjustment
    • Our margin (we explain it rather than hide it)
    • The number we'd wire to you at closing
    • The closing date window

    No pressure to decide on the call. Take it to family, an attorney, or your own agent.

  4. 4. Close on the date you choose

    If you accept, we open escrow with a St. Joseph County, Elkhart County, or Berrien County (MI) title company. You pick the closing date — typically 14–30 days out, sometimes as fast as 7 days if you're up against a deadline.

    At closing, you sign. Funds wired to your bank that day. We pay all closing costs.
