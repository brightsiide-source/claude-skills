Why Mishawaka Homeowners Sell to Us

Mishawaka is South Bend's twin city — about 50,000 residents along the St. Joseph River with a distinct identity. The seller patterns we see most:

  • Bayer / AM General / Bosch employee relocations. Mishawaka's manufacturing employers move people in and out on tight timelines.
  • University Park Mall-area retail and office moves — retail managers, regional sales reps, and similar mobile workforce.
  • Estate sales from longtime Mishawaka residents — properties typically built 1940s-1970s with original systems and decades of deferred maintenance.
  • Downtown Mishawaka equity sellers taking gains from the riverfront-revitalization wave.
  • Pre-foreclosure situations. Indiana is a judicial foreclosure state — Mishawaka cases go through St. Joseph Superior Court, roughly 150-180 days from filing to sheriff's sale.
  • Tired landlords with single-family rentals along the Lincolnway corridor.
  • Distressed properties — flood-zone properties, basement water issues common to riverfront proximity, lead-paint disclosures.
