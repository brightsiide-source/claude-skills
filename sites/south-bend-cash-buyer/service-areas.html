Where We Buy: The Michiana Area.

St. Joseph County (IN), Elkhart County (IN), and Berrien County (MI). From South Bend to Elkhart to the Michigan state line.

Primary Cities

Cities with dedicated landing pages and detailed local context:

South Bend

ZIPs 46601-46637. St. Joseph County seat. Notre Dame to the north, Beacon Health, manufacturing base. River Park, Edison Park, Sunnymede, Near Northwest.

Mishawaka

ZIPs 46544-46546. Twin city to South Bend along the St. Joseph River. Battell Park, downtown, Toscana Park, University Park Mall area.

Granger

ZIPs 46530, 46545. Affluent unincorporated community in Penn Township. Strong school district. Heritage Square area, Knollwood.

Elkhart

ZIPs 46514-46517. RV capital of the world — Forest River, Coachmen, Thor, plus suppliers. Manufacturing-driven.

Niles, MI

ZIP 49120. Just over the Michigan state line, in Berrien County. Quiet residential, many South Bend / Mishawaka commuters.

Osceola

ZIP 46561. Small town between Mishawaka and Elkhart along the US-12 corridor.

Roseland

Tiny town directly north of South Bend, adjacent to Notre Dame.
