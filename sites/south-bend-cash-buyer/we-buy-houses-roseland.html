Why Roseland Homeowners Sell to Us

Roseland is unusual — a small incorporated town of about 700 residents adjacent to Notre Dame, with its own town government but tightly interwoven with South Bend's north side and the Notre Dame area. Common seller patterns:

  • Notre Dame-adjacent property exits. Many Roseland homes are within walking distance of Notre Dame's campus and have been used as faculty housing, student housing, or game-day rentals.
  • Estate sales from longtime residents — small housing stock means most homes are 50+ years old with significant deferred maintenance.
  • Out-of-state inheritances from Notre Dame retirees whose adult children live elsewhere.
  • Tired landlords with rental properties that became too much management work.
  • Pre-foreclosure situations in St. Joseph County.
  • Distressed properties — frozen-pipe damage, foundation issues, code violations on the older housing stock.
