FAQ

Common Questions, Honest Answers.

The questions we get most often from Michiana-area sellers, answered without sales-speak.

The Process

How fast can you buy my house in South Bend?

As fast as 7 days from offer acceptance. Most closings happen 14–30 days out at a local St. Joseph County title company. You pick the date.

How do you calculate the offer?

We pull recent sold comps in your specific Michiana neighborhood, weight them by recency, distance, size, and condition, and factor in estimated repair costs and our margin. The written offer shows the math.

Is the offer really no-obligation?

Yes. The offer is typically good for 7 days. Plenty of sellers take it to a local agent for a second opinion and list instead.

What's the catch?

Honest catch: our offer will generally be below what a fully-staged, fully-inspected MLS listing might fetch over 90 days. You're trading the price difference for speed, certainty, no commission, no closing costs, no repair credits.

Money

Are there any fees?

No. No commission, no closing costs, no inspection fees, no repair credits. The offer number is the number wired to your bank at closing.

What if I owe more than the house is worth?

If significantly underwater, a short sale negotiated with your lender is usually better than a cash sale. We'll tell you that on the first call.

Property Condition

Do I need to clean or make repairs?

No. We buy as-is. Leave behind anything you don't want.

What if my house is in really rough shape?

Rough shape is our specialty. Fire, frozen-pipe, foundation, hoarding, vacant homes — we buy them.

Do you buy properties with tenants?

Yes. We buy tenant-occupied properties, even with difficult tenants.

Specific Situations

What if I'm in foreclosure?

Indiana is a judicial foreclosure state — St. Joseph Superior Court handles cases, typical timeline 150–180 days. We can usually close before sheriff's sale. See foreclosure.

What if I inherited the house?

We work with executors (personal representatives) and out-of-state heirs. Indiana probate (Title 29) typically runs 4–8 months for unsupervised cases. See inherited-house page.

What if I'm going through a divorce?

Indiana is equitable distribution. We can sell the marital home with both spouses' agreement; proceeds split per the decree. See divorce sale page.

What if I'm out of state?

The entire transaction can be handled remotely via overnight mail or remote online notarization.

About Us

Who actually buys the house?

South Bend Cash Home Buyers is locally operated. We use our own funds. Some properties we rent, some we renovate and resell, some we hold.

Are you a real estate agent?

No. We're not realtors. We don't list properties on the MLS.

What areas of Michiana do you serve?

South Bend, Mishawaka, Granger, Elkhart, Niles MI, Osceola, Roseland, plus surrounding St. Joseph, Elkhart, and Berrien county communities. Full list on our service-areas page.
