About Us

Local Cash Buyers. Real Numbers. No Games.

South Bend Cash Home Buyers is a locally-operated home-buying business serving the entire Michiana area. We buy houses with our own funds, write fair offers backed by real comps, and close on the date you choose — usually within 30 days.

Why We Started South Bend Cash Home Buyers

The traditional way of selling a house works fine for some people. Get the place spotless. Pay an agent 5-6%. Show it for weeks. Negotiate inspection credits. Pray financing doesn't fall through. Pay closing costs. Move out on the buyer's timeline.

For a lot of Michiana-area homeowners — the people inheriting a parent's house in Sunnymede, the people facing a sheriff's sale in St. Joseph Superior Court, the families whose Granger equity is locked up while they need cash for a relocation, the RV-industry workers caught in an Elkhart layoff cycle — that process either takes too long, costs too much, or both.

We started South Bend Cash Home Buyers to be the alternative. Not the right choice for every seller, but the obviously right choice for some. The promise is simple: a fair number, written down, that we actually close on. No 11th-hour renegotiations. No commission deducted at closing. No "the buyer's lender wants another inspection."

How We're Different from a Realtor

  • We are the buyer. A realtor finds you a buyer. We use our own funds — no bank financing, no contingencies, no waiting on someone else's approval.
  • We pay all closing costs. The number we quote is the number wired to you at closing.
  • We buy as-is. Don't fix the roof. Don't paint. Don't even clean it out — we'll handle whatever's left.
  • We close on your date. 7 days, 14 days, 45 days — whatever works.
  • We're transparent about the tradeoff. Our offer is generally below what a fully-staged MLS listing might fetch over 90 days. We show the math.

If you have time, a clean property, and patience, an agent will probably get you a higher gross number. If you need certainty, speed, or simplicity — that's what we're for.

Our Process, Step by Step

  1. 1. You reach out

    Call, text, or fill out a form. Tell us the address and a little about the situation. We respond same day.

  2. 2. We do the research

    We pull recent sold comps in your specific Michiana neighborhood, factor in condition, recent improvements, and what comparable area homes closed at in the last 90 days.

  3. 3. Written cash offer

    Within 24 hours of research, you get a written, no-obligation offer with the math shown — comps, condition adjustments, our margin.

  4. 4. You pick the close date

    We coordinate with a St. Joseph County, Elkhart County, or Berrien County title company. You choose the closing date. Funds wired at closing.

What We're Not

We're not realtors. We don't have a brokerage license. We don't list houses on the MLS. We are professional home buyers who purchase houses with our own funds for our own portfolio — some we rent, some we renovate and resell, some we hold.

This means we're not the right fit if your goal is to maximize the gross sale price and you have the time, condition, and patience for an MLS listing. If your goal is certainty, speed, or selling as-is — that's us.
