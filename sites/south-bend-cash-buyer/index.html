FAQ

Common Questions

How fast can you actually close?

As fast as 7 days for clean title transactions. Most sellers choose to close in 14–30 days. Because we pay cash, there’s no lender, no appraisal, and no underwriting holdups.

Do I really pay nothing in fees or closing costs?

Correct. Our offer is the amount you walk away with at closing. We cover title fees, transfer taxes, recording fees — the works. There are no agent commissions either.

What kind of houses do you buy?

Single family, duplexes, multi-family up to 4 units, and most condos in the South Bend / Mishawaka / Elkhart area. Any condition — vacant, occupied, tenant-occupied, fire damaged, hoarder homes, you name it.

How do you decide what to offer?

We look at recent comparable sales in your neighborhood, the current condition of the property, and what it will cost us to renovate it. Then we work backwards to an offer that’s fair to both of us. We’ll walk you through the numbers.

Are you a real estate agent?

No. We’re direct cash buyers and a wholesale real estate company. We are not listing your home — we are making you a cash offer to buy it ourselves or assign the contract to a vetted investor partner.

What if I’m behind on the mortgage or in pre-foreclosure?

That’s one of the most common reasons people call us. We can often close before a sale date and pay off the bank directly. Don’t wait — the sooner you reach out, the more options you have.

Is there any obligation if I get an offer?

None. The offer is free and you can take it, leave it, or use it as a benchmark when comparing other options.
