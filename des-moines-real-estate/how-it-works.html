How It Works

Selling Your House to Us, Step by Step.

Four steps. No commission, no closing costs, no contingencies. Close on a date you pick — typically within 30 days.

The Four Steps

  1. 1. Tell us about the property

    Call, text, or fill out a form on any page of this site. We need three pieces of information to start: the address, a rough sense of the condition, and your timeline. That's it. Most initial calls take under five minutes. We don't ask for a Social Security number, a credit check, or proof of ownership at this stage — those come later, at closing, handled by the title company.

  2. 2. We pull Des Moines-area comps and assess condition

    Within 24 hours of your call, we pull recent sold comparables for your specific neighborhood — not city-wide averages. A Beaverdale Tudor doesn't trade like a Highland Park ranch, and Prairie Trail comps don't predict downtown Ankeny prices. We weight comps by recency (last 90 days preferred), distance, square footage, and condition.

    Sometimes we walk the property in person before making an offer; sometimes a thorough photo set from you is enough. We'll tell you which we need.

  3. 3. Written cash offer in 24 hours

    You get a written offer — not a verbal range, not "we'll get back to you." The offer document shows:

    • The comps we used
    • Our condition adjustment (and what it's based on)
    • Our margin (we explain this rather than hide it)
    • The number we'd wire to you at closing
    • The closing date window

    There's no pressure to decide on the call. Take it to a family member, attorney, or your own agent. Many sellers do. We'd rather you say yes after thinking about it than yes under pressure that you regret.

  4. 4. Close on the date you choose

    If you accept, we open escrow with a local Polk County title company. You pick the closing date — typically 14–30 days out, sometimes as fast as 7 days if you're up against a deadline (foreclosure, relocation, family situation).

    At closing, the title company handles the paperwork. You sign. Funds are wired to your bank that day. We pay all closing costs — the number on the offer is the number you receive.

Common Questions About the Process

Most callers have the same handful of questions. Quick answers below; full answers on our FAQ page.

  • Is the offer really no-obligation? Yes. We've had sellers take our offer to an agent and list instead. No hard feelings.
  • Do you charge any fees? No. The offer is what hits your bank account. We pay all closing costs.
  • What if the house is in really bad shape? That's our specialty. Fire damage, code violations, hoarding, foundation issues — we buy as-is. See our as-is page.
  • What if I'm in foreclosure? Iowa is a judicial foreclosure state with a 5–7 month timeline in Polk County. We can usually close before the sheriff's sale. See our foreclosure page.
  • What if I'm out of state? The whole process can be handled remotely. Documents via overnight mail or remote online notarization.
