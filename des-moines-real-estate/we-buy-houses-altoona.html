Why Altoona Homeowners Sell to Us

Altoona is the largest of the eastern Des Moines suburbs — a more middle-market, working-family community with a wide mix of older established neighborhoods and newer construction. Common seller patterns we see:

  • Hospitality and gaming workers. Prairie Meadows and Adventureland are major employers. Seasonal turnover, transfers, and shift changes all drive relocation.
  • Hy-Vee corporate staff. Hy-Vee's headquarters relocations in/out of the metro often require fast closes.
  • Tornado and storm damage. Eastern Polk County has had more than its share of severe weather. We buy properties with hail, wind, or water damage that insurance hasn't covered or that owners don't want to repair before selling.
  • Pre-foreclosure situations. Working-family markets see more financial stress cycles. We close before sheriff's sale to preserve credit and any remaining equity.
  • Estate sales of mid-century homes near 1st Street and the older Altoona grid — properties that often need significant work before they'd show well on the MLS.
  • Tired landlords with single-family rentals along the 8th Street corridor.
