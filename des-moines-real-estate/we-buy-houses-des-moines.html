Why Des Moines Homeowners Sell to Us

Des Moines proper has one of the most diverse housing stocks in the Midwest — from 1900s Sherman Hill mansions, to mid-century Beaverdale Tudors, to East Village lofts, to working-class neighborhoods south of downtown. That diversity means we hear from sellers in just about every situation:

  • Principal Financial, Wells Fargo, and Nationwide relocations. Des Moines is a corporate-finance hub. When employees get transferred, relocation packages rarely match a 90-day MLS timeline. We close in 14-30 days.
  • Beaverdale and Sherman Hill estate sales. Older bungalows passing to adult children — properties with charm but knob-and-tube wiring, original kitchens, and roof issues that need to be priced into any offer.
  • Pre-foreclosure situations in Polk County. Iowa's judicial foreclosure timeline gives us 5-7 months — usually enough time to close before sheriff's sale and preserve credit + equity. See our foreclosure page for details.
  • Inherited houses caught in probate. We work with the executor or adult-child heir on whatever timeline the court allows. See our inherited-house page.
  • Tired landlords with single-family rentals in Drake, Highland Park, the South Side, or East Des Moines that have become more management work than they're worth.
  • Divorce sales where both parties want a clean, quick close. See our divorce sale page.
  • Distressed properties — fire damage, code violations, hoarding situations, foundation problems. We buy as-is. See our as-is page.
  • Out-of-state owners who inherited or bought a property remotely and want to liquidate without flying back for inspections.
