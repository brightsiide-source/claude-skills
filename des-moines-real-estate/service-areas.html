Where We Buy: The Des Moines Metro.

Polk, Dallas, and Warren counties. From downtown Des Moines to the fastest-growing edges of the metro. Eight primary city pages, plus a dozen smaller communities we cover.

Primary Cities

These are the cities with dedicated landing pages and detailed local context — ZIPs, neighborhood breakdowns, and the seller situations we see most in each market.

Des Moines

ZIPs 50309–50327. From Beaverdale Tudors to Sherman Hill bungalows to East Village condos to Highland Park ranches. The full housing-stock range — we buy in all of it.

West Des Moines

ZIPs 50265, 50266. Jordan Creek, Valley Junction, West Glen, Country Club. Dense with corporate-HQ employees — Wells Fargo, Hy-Vee, Wellmark, Athene.

Ankeny

ZIPs 50021, 50023. Fastest-growing suburb in the Iowa metro. Prairie Trail, Otter Creek, downtown Ankeny — mix of new construction and 1960s-era ranches.

Urbandale

ZIPs 50322, 50323. Aurora Hills, Walker-Johnston, Karen Acres, historic downtown Urbandale at 97th & Douglas. Lots of 1960s/70s housing stock.

Johnston

ZIP 50131. Camp Dodge / Iowa National Guard area, Beaver Creek, NW 62nd corridor. Small but affluent, military-adjacent.

Waukee

ZIP 50263. Fastest-growing city in Iowa. Kettlestone, Glynn Village, tech-employer-driven (Microsoft data center, expanding corporate corridor).

Clive

ZIP 50325. Small, established, professional-family suburb tucked between West Des Moines and Urbandale. Mostly 1970s–2000s housing stock.

Altoona

ZIP 50009. East-metro working-family community. Adventureland and Prairie Meadows nearby; Hy-Vee corporate is a major employer.
