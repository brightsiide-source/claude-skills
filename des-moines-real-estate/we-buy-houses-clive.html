Why Clive Homeowners Sell to Us

Clive is a small, established, professional-family suburb sandwiched between West Des Moines and Urbandale. Most of the housing stock dates from the 1970s through early 2000s, with a long-tenured ownership base. Common seller patterns:

  • Long-term owners downsizing. 30+ year residents whose Clive ranch is paid off but who now want a maintenance-free condo elsewhere. They want certainty, not a six-month listing process.
  • Estate sales of mid-century homes near the Greenbelt — properties with mature trees, decent bones, and 30 years of deferred maintenance.
  • Professional-family moves tied to law firm partnerships, hospital placements (Mercy, MercyOne, UnityPoint), and Wells Fargo corporate transfers in/out of the metro.
  • Equity-rich sellers looking to take cash out without staging or showings. Clive has appreciated steadily, and many owners have substantial equity.
  • Investor exits from the rental properties that exist along the borders with Urbandale and WDM.
  • Pre-divorce or post-divorce sales where speed and clean closure matter.
