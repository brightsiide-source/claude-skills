Why West Des Moines Homeowners Sell to Us

West Des Moines is the largest and one of the most affluent suburbs in the Des Moines metro — which means most sellers here aren't in distress. But "I have time" doesn't always mean "I want to spend three months on an MLS listing." We close regularly on:

  • Corporate relocations from Wells Fargo, Hy-Vee corporate, Wellmark, and Athene. WDM is dense with HQs and regional offices. When relocation packages don't cover a 90-day listing process, we cover the gap with a 14-30 day close.
  • Equity-rich downsizers in Country Club, West Glen, and the older streets off Grand Avenue who want to lock in equity without staging, professional photography, and weeks of showings.
  • Valley Junction estate sales. Many of the historic homes south of Railroad Avenue are passing to adult children — properties with charm but also 50-year-old systems, knob-and-tube wiring, and deferred maintenance that make traditional sales complicated.
  • Empty-nesters moving to maintenance-free condos. They want to close on the old house the same week they close on the new one. We can sync timelines.
  • Investor exits from rental properties near Jordan Creek that have appreciated significantly but no longer cash-flow at current rates.
  • Pre-divorce or pre-litigation sales where speed and certainty matter more than maximum price.

Honest note: WDM has one of the more active resale markets in Iowa. If you have time, a clean property, and patience, listing with an agent will likely fetch a higher gross number. Our offers are most compelling when speed, certainty, or condition make MLS impractical.
