Why Ankeny Homeowners Sell to Us

Ankeny has been one of the fastest-growing cities in Iowa for a decade. That growth brings a wide range of seller situations — not just the "I need cash fast" stories. We close regularly on properties for owners dealing with:

  • John Deere and corporate relocations. Ankeny's largest employer moves engineers and ag-tech staff in and out on tight timelines. When relocation packages don't cover a 60-day MLS process, we cover the gap.
  • Estate sales of mid-century homes near downtown Ankeny and the older streets off SW State Street — properties with deferred maintenance that adult children inherited but don't have time to renovate before listing.
  • Prairie Trail and Otter Creek equity sellers. Newer master-planned homes that have appreciated significantly since 2018. Some owners want to lock in equity quickly without staging, showings, or competing offers falling through on inspection.
  • Pre-foreclosure and tax-lien situations. Polk County's judicial foreclosure timeline is long enough that there's usually time to sell before a sheriff's sale — but only if you don't go the traditional MLS route. We've closed in as few as 10 days when the deadline was real.
  • Divorce and life-change sales. Neither party wants to spend three months prepping a house for showings during an emotionally hard period. We give a number, both parties review it, and we close on a date that works for both.
  • Tired landlords with rental properties near 1st Street or the Briarwood area that have become more management work than they're worth.

The common thread: certainty matters more than maximum price. We don't try to hide that. The number we quote will usually be below what a fully-staged, fully-inspected MLS listing might fetch over 90 days — but it's a cash offer with no contingencies, no commission, no closing costs, and a closing date you pick.
