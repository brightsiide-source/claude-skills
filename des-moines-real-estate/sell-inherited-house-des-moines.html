What to Know About Iowa Probate

In Iowa, most estates with real property go through probate in district court — in the Des Moines metro, that's typically Polk County District Court. The process is governed by Iowa Code Title XV (probate code).

A few practical points that matter for selling:

  • Most Iowa probates take 6-12 months. Simple, uncontested estates can move faster; contested or complex estates take longer.
  • The executor (called a "personal representative" in Iowa) can usually list and sell the house before probate fully closes — but the sale itself typically requires court approval, and the proceeds usually go through the estate account, not directly to heirs.
  • You don't have to clean it out. We buy properties with the deceased's belongings still in them. Take family photos, jewelry, important documents, and anything sentimental. Leave the rest.
  • Out-of-state heirs are common in Iowa estate sales — lots of Iowa-raised adults now live elsewhere and don't want to fly back for showings, inspections, or cleanouts. We can handle the entire transaction remotely if needed; document signing can be done via overnight mail or remote-online notarization.

If you're not sure where the estate is in the process, that's fine — tell us on the call and we'll point you toward what needs to happen next.

Why Inherited Houses Don't Sell Well on the MLS

Some inherited houses are perfect candidates for a traditional listing — well-maintained, recently updated, in a desirable neighborhood. Most aren't. Common issues we see in Des Moines-metro estate sales:

  • Deferred maintenance. Owner stayed too long; the roof, HVAC, and kitchen all need work that nobody got around to.
  • Original 1960s/70s/80s finishes. Brown linoleum, wood paneling, original cabinets — buyers want them gone, and you'd need $20k-$60k of work to make the house competitive.
  • Stuff. Forty years of life accumulated in a house. The MLS process expects an empty, clean home.
  • Multi-heir disagreements. Three siblings can't agree on price, paint color, or whether to list. A cash sale gives a number all three can vote yes or no on.
  • Out-of-state executors who can't easily coordinate with agents, inspectors, and cleaners from another time zone.
