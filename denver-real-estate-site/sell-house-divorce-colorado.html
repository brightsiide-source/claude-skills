How Colorado Divorce Affects the House

Colorado is an equitable distribution state — not community property. The court divides marital assets fairly (which doesn't necessarily mean 50/50), considering factors like each spouse's contribution, economic circumstances, and the desirability of awarding the family home to the custodial parent.

There's a mandatory 91-day waiting period from the date the petition is served before a Colorado dissolution can be finalized.

The marital home is usually the largest single asset, and how it's handled typically follows one of three paths:

  1. One spouse buys out the other and keeps the house. Usually requires a refinance to remove the other spouse from the mortgage, plus a cash settlement.
  2. Both spouses agree to sell and split proceeds per the Separation Agreement. Usually MLS-listed — 60-90 days.
  3. Cash sale to a third party for speed and certainty. Both parties sign at closing; proceeds split per the agreement; both walk away same day.

We're typically Path 3 — the cash buyer.

Honest Note About Price
