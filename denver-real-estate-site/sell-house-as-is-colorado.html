The Kinds of Properties We Buy in Colorado

  • Fire-damaged houses. The Marshall Fire and other urban-wildfire events have left many properties in various states of damage. We buy them as they sit.
  • Hail and storm damage. Colorado's Front Range gets some of the most destructive hail in the country. Roof damage, siding damage, broken windows — we buy with or without insurance claims pending.
  • Foundation issues from clay soils. Colorado's bentonite and expansive clay soils cause settling, cracking, and shifting. We factor it into the offer rather than demand repair credits at closing.
  • Code enforcement situations. City of Denver, Aurora, Lakewood, and county code citations.
  • Hoarding properties. No judgment. Leave everything. We do the cleanout.
  • Long-vacant houses. Owner died, family moved, house sat for years.
  • Mid-rehab houses. Started a flip during the 2020-2021 boom, ran out of money.
  • Tenant-occupied disasters. Bad tenants, lease complications, properties trashed.
  • Mountain-area properties with snow-load damage, frozen-pipe damage, off-grid complications.

How As-Is Pricing Actually Works

Our offer is based on the property's after-repair value (ARV) — what it would sell for once renovated — minus our estimated rehab cost and a margin. So a distressed property in Denver with $90k of needed repairs and a $550k ARV might net you something in the $340k-$380k range as a cash offer.

The condition doesn't change whether we'll buy. The condition does change what we can pay — because we're the ones doing the rehab.

We're transparent about this math. On the call, we'll show our ARV estimate, our rehab estimate, and how we got to the offer.

Honest Note: When MLS Beats As-Is Cash

If the issue is cosmetic — outdated kitchen, old carpet, dated bathrooms — you may be better off with a traditional listing. Investors compete hard for "needs cosmetic updates" houses in Denver, Boulder, and Fort Collins.

If the issue is structural, environmental, or legal — that's our lane. MLS buyers' financing falls apart on those.
