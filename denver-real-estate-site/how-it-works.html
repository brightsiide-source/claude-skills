How It Works

Selling Your House to Us, Step by Step.

Four steps. No commission, no closing costs, no contingencies. Close on a date you pick — typically within 30 days.

The Four Steps

  1. 1. Tell us about the property

    Call, text, or fill out a form. We need three pieces of information: the address, a rough sense of the condition, and your timeline. Most calls take under five minutes.

  2. 2. We pull Colorado comps and assess condition

    Within 24 hours, we pull recent sold comparables for your specific submarket. A Park Hill 1920s craftsman doesn't trade like a Highlands Ranch newer build; Fort Collins doesn't predict Greeley. We weight comps by recency, distance, size, and condition.

  3. 3. Written cash offer in 24 hours

    You get a written offer with comps used, condition adjustment, our margin, the number we'd wire to you at closing, and the closing date window. No pressure to decide.

  4. 4. Close on the date you choose

    We open escrow with a Denver, Larimer, Weld, or Boulder County title company. You pick the closing date — typically 14–30 days out, sometimes as fast as 7. Funds wired to your bank that day.
