Why Boulder Homeowners Sell to Us

Boulder is one of the most affluent and tightly-zoned housing markets in Colorado — strict growth management policies have kept supply constrained for decades. Most Boulder sellers aren't in distress, but the patterns we see:

  • CU Boulder faculty & staff relocations. Sabbaticals, tenure denials, retirements, inter-university moves drive Boulder turnover.
  • NCAR, NIST, NOAA federal-research transfers. Federal scientific employers move researchers and managers regularly.
  • Tech-industry equity sellers. Boulder's tech scene (Google, IBM, Tech4Good, plus dozens of startups) generates lots of equity-rich sellers who want to lock in gains.
  • Estate sales from longtime Boulder residents — many older homes in Mapleton Hill and Newlands have been in the same family for 40+ years.
  • Marshall Fire and other wildfire-damage exits. Properties near the Foothills that have been damaged or are deemed insurance-uninsurable.
  • Pre-divorce sales where confidentiality and speed matter more than maximum gross.
  • Investor exits from rental properties near campus.

Honest note: Boulder has one of the most competitive resale markets in the country thanks to supply constraints. If your home is in good shape and you have time, a traditional listing will almost always fetch more. Our offers are most compelling when speed, certainty, or condition make MLS impractical.
