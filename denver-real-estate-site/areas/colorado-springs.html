Why Colorado Springs Homeowners Sell to Us

Colorado Springs is the second-largest city in Colorado — about 480,000 residents in El Paso County. The market is dominated by military, Olympic Training Center workforce, and a steady inflow of remote workers from the Front Range. Common seller patterns:

  • Military PCS relocations. Fort Carson, U.S. Air Force Academy, Peterson Space Force Base, Schriever SFB, and Cheyenne Mountain all drive constant turnover. We close on PCS timelines, including remote closings via mobile notary at any new duty station.
  • Out-of-state inheritances. Many Colorado Springs homes were bought by Boomer retirees; their adult children typically live elsewhere.
  • Estate sales of older homes in Old Colorado City, Westside, and Patty Jewett — significant deferred maintenance.
  • Hail damage and insurance disputes. The Front Range hail corridor runs right through El Paso County; many properties have unresolved roof, siding, or window claims.
  • Pre-foreclosure situations. Colorado's Public Trustee process runs 110-125 days; we can close before the auction.
  • Tired landlords with rentals near Fort Carson or downtown.
  • Distressed properties — foundation issues from expansive clay soils, code citations, off-grid issues on Black Forest and Manitou Springs acreage.
