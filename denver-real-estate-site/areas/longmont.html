Cash Home Buyers Serving All of Longmont

Longmont straddles the line between Boulder County's premium real estate market and the more accessible Northern Colorado corridor. With a vibrant downtown anchored by Main Street's restaurants and breweries, plus proximity to Boulder and Denver via the Diagonal Highway, Longmont attracts buyers at every price point. But for homeowners whose properties need significant work, selling traditionally in this market means competing against move-in-ready listings and paying Boulder County's higher agent fees.

We buy homes across all Longmont zip codes — from the 80501 neighborhoods near Downtown and Garden Acres to the 80504 developments east of Main Street near Fox Hill and Prospect. Many Longmont homes were built during the 1970s and 1980s housing boom and now face issues like polybutylene plumbing, aging electrical panels, and worn-out roofing. These are exactly the properties we specialize in purchasing.

Longmont was also significantly impacted by the 2013 floods, and some properties still carry lingering issues — basement moisture problems, grading challenges, and foundation concerns. If your Longmont home has flood history, deferred maintenance, or any other condition that makes a traditional sale difficult, we'll still make you a fair cash offer. Our team has been active in the Longmont market for years, and we understand the nuances that affect property values in every neighborhood from Sunset to Hover Acres.
