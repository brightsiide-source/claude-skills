Why Aurora Homeowners Sell to Us

Aurora is Colorado's third-largest city — about 400,000 residents spread across Arapahoe, Adams, and Douglas counties. The housing stock and seller situations vary widely by submarket:

  • Buckley Space Force Base (formerly Buckley AFB) military moves. PCS orders move people in and out on tight timelines.
  • Anschutz Medical Campus relocations. UCHealth, Children's Hospital Colorado, and the surrounding medical district move physicians, nurses, and researchers regularly.
  • Original Aurora estate sales. Many of the 1940s-1960s homes in the original parts of town are passing to adult children with significant deferred maintenance.
  • Southlands and Tower Road newer-build sellers taking equity from the 2018-2022 appreciation wave.
  • Pre-foreclosure situations. Colorado's Public Trustee process runs 110-125 days; we close before the auction.
  • Out-of-state inheritances from Aurora retirees with adult children elsewhere.
  • Distressed properties — hail damage common along the Front Range, foundation issues from clay soils, code citations from the City of Aurora.
