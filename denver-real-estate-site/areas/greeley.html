Cash Home Buyers Serving All of Greeley

Greeley is the seat of Weld County and one of Northern Colorado's most affordable housing markets — but affordability doesn't always mean easy selling. As a university town anchored by the University of Northern Colorado, Greeley has a mix of student rentals near campus, established family homes in neighborhoods like Glenmere Park and Sunrise Park, and newer development pushing west toward Windsor and Highway 34. Many older properties in the 80631 and 80634 zip codes carry deferred maintenance that makes traditional listing a challenge.

We buy houses across Greeley regardless of condition. Whether it's a worn-out rental property near 10th Avenue, a 1960s ranch in Farr Park with an outdated kitchen and original plumbing, or a Hillside home with a cracked foundation from Weld County's expansive soils, we make fair cash offers with no contingencies. Greeley's agricultural heritage means many properties sit on larger lots with outbuildings, old wells, or septic systems — factors that scare off conventional buyers but don't faze us.

Our team has been buying homes in Greeley and across Weld County since 2018. We know this market well — from the revitalizing Downtown Greeley corridor to the established Westmoor subdivisions on the west side. If your Greeley property needs more work than it's worth investing in, or if you simply want the speed and certainty of a cash sale, we're ready to make you an offer.
