About Us

Local Cash Buyers. Real Numbers. No Games.

Colorado Cash Home Buyers is a locally-operated home-buying business serving the Denver metro and Northern Colorado. We buy houses with our own funds, write fair offers backed by real comps, and close on the date you choose — usually within 30 days.

Why We Started Colorado Cash Home Buyers

The traditional way of selling a house works fine for some people. Get the place spotless. Pay an agent 5-6%. Show it for weeks. Negotiate inspection credits. Pray financing doesn't fall through. Pay closing costs. Move out on the buyer's timeline.

For a lot of Colorado homeowners — the people inheriting a parent's house in Park Hill or Lakewood, the people facing a Public Trustee foreclosure sale in Denver County, the families whose Fort Collins equity is locked up while they need cash for a relocation, the Boulder owners of fire-damaged or flood-damaged properties an MLS buyer's lender won't touch — that process either takes too long, costs too much, or both.

We started Colorado Cash Home Buyers to be the alternative. Not the right choice for every seller, but the obviously right choice for some. The promise is simple: a fair number, written down, that we actually close on. No 11th-hour renegotiations. No commission deducted at closing.

How We're Different from a Realtor

  • We are the buyer. A realtor finds you a buyer. We use our own funds — no bank financing, no contingencies.
  • We pay all closing costs. The number we quote is the number wired to you at closing.
  • We buy as-is. Don't fix the roof. Don't paint. Don't even clean it out.
  • We close on your date. 7 days, 14 days, 45 days — whatever works.
  • We're transparent about the tradeoff. Our offer is generally below what a fully-staged MLS listing might fetch over 90 days. We show the math.

If you have time, a clean property, and patience, an agent will probably get you a higher gross number. If you need certainty, speed, or simplicity — that's what we're for.

Our Process, Step by Step

  1. 1. You reach out

    Call, text, or fill out a form. We respond same day.

  2. 2. We do the research

    We pull recent sold comps in your specific Colorado neighborhood, factor in condition, recent improvements, and what comparable homes closed at in the last 90 days.

  3. 3. Written cash offer

    Within 24 hours, you get a written, no-obligation offer with the math shown.

  4. 4. You pick the close date

    We coordinate with a Denver, Larimer, Weld, or Boulder County title company. Funds wired at closing.

What We're Not

We're not realtors. We don't have a brokerage license. We don't list houses on the MLS. We are professional home buyers who purchase houses with our own funds for our own portfolio — some we rent, some we renovate and resell, some we hold.

This means we're not the right fit if your goal is to maximize the gross sale price and you have the time, condition, and patience for an MLS listing. If your goal is certainty, speed, or selling as-is — that's us.
