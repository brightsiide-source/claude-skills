# Outreach Best Practices

Cold outreach to small businesses works when it is **specific, short, and respectful of the recipient's time and inbox**. The drafts this skill generates are starting points — edit pass matters more than template quality.

## The 30-second rule

A local business owner reads your email on a phone, between customers, in under 30 seconds. If your first sentence doesn't make it obvious you looked at their specific business, you are deleted.

- ✅ "I was looking at [site] this morning and noticed…"
- ❌ "We help businesses like yours grow…"

## Structure that converts

1. **Why I'm writing** (1 sentence, specific to them)
2. **What you noticed** (1-3 bullets of concrete findings)
3. **What you do** (1 sentence — the offer, not the pitch)
4. **Soft CTA** (10-minute call, or "can I send examples?")
5. **Signature** with phone number

## Response-rate multipliers

- **Mention the city or neighborhood** by name
- **Reference one specific competitor** who does have a modern site ("Like [local competitor]'s site")
- **Attach a 30-second screen recording** walking through the issues (Loom, free tier)
- **Offer a no-strings audit** rather than a sales call
- **Send mid-morning local time**, avoid Mondays and Fridays

## What kills response rate

- "I hope this email finds you well"
- Buzzwords: synergy, solutions, best-in-class
- Long paragraphs
- A call-to-action that asks for too much ("30-minute demo")
- PDF or HTML attachments (triggers spam filters)
- Images in the body of a cold email (same reason)

## Compliance basics

### US — CAN-SPAM
- Accurate "From" and subject line
- A physical mailing address in the footer
- Clear, honorable opt-out ("just reply STOP" works for SMS; include an unsubscribe link or plain "reply and I'll remove you")
- Honor opt-outs within 10 business days

### Canada — CASL
- You need **express** or **implied** consent. A public business listing with an email is implied consent for a related commercial message **for 6 months**
- Full name + mailing address + easy opt-out required

### EU / UK — GDPR + PECR
- B2B cold email to a **generic business address** (info@, contact@) is generally allowed under legitimate interest
- B2B cold email to a **named individual's work email** requires you to be able to justify legitimate interest and have a balancing test on file
- Never email a personal address (gmail/hotmail/yahoo) cold

### Phone / SMS
- Check your country's do-not-call registry before calling
- SMS: explicit opt-in is safer; even cold SMS requires a clear opt-out mechanism
- Keep voicemails under 25 seconds

## Volume guidance

Small, hand-reviewed batches perform better than large blasts:

- 10-20 **personalized** emails/day → 15-25% response rate
- 100+ **templated** emails/day → <1% response rate, plus deliverability damage

When in doubt: send fewer, better.

## Follow-up cadence

Most replies come on the 2nd or 3rd touch, not the 1st.

| Day | Touch | Channel |
|-----|-------|---------|
| 0   | Initial email | Email |
| 3   | Short nudge, add a new data point | Email |
| 8   | Different channel | Voicemail or SMS |
| 15  | Final: "closing the loop" | Email |

Three touches total is usually enough. If no reply by touch 3, move on.

## Ethical floor

- Do not misrepresent who you are or where you got their information
- Do not use urgency tactics ("your site will be blacklisted")
- Do not deepfake a referral ("John said to reach out") you don't have
- If they say no, say thank you and stop — don't re-add them to a drip
