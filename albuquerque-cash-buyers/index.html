FAQ

Common questions, straight answers.

How fast can you actually close?

Seven days if title is clean and you're motivated. Most of our Albuquerque closings happen in two to three weeks. You set the date — we'll match it.

How do you decide what to offer?

We pull recent comparable sales in your specific ABQ neighborhood, estimate repairs to bring it to market condition, and back out our holding and transaction costs. The number we send is the number we close at — no last-minute renegotiation.

Are there really no fees?

None. No commissions, no transaction fees, no junk fees. We pay standard seller closing costs. The number on your offer is the number you net (minus any existing mortgage payoff).

Do I need to clean or fix anything?

Nope. Leave the broken appliances, the storage unit's worth of stuff in the garage, the carpet you've been meaning to replace. We handle it.

Will you buy with tenants in place?

Yes — occupied, vacant, Section 8, behind on rent, month-to-month. Tell us the situation, we'll structure around it.

What if I'm in foreclosure or behind on the mortgage?

Call today, not tomorrow. New Mexico is a judicial foreclosure state, which buys you some time, but the clock is ticking. We can often close fast enough to stop the auction.

Is there any obligation when I request an offer?

Zero. You get a written cash offer. Take it, leave it, or use it as leverage with an agent. No follow-up spam.

Are you a wholesaler or do you actually buy?

Both, depending on the deal. Some properties we keep as rentals, some we renovate and resell, some we assign to a vetted partner buyer. Either way, you get a written offer, you sign one contract, and you close. That's all that changes for you.
