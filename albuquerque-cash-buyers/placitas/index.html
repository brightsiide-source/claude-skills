We Buy Houses in Placitas, NM — For Cash.

Placitas is a foothills community in Sandoval County, nestled at the base of the Sandia Mountains north of Albuquerque off I-25. Custom homes on acreage, view properties, mountain-adjacent living. We pay cash, close in 7-30 days, and cover all closing costs.

Why Placitas Homeowners Sell to Us

  • Retiree downsizing. Many Placitas owners are 60+ and ready to move closer to family or into easier-to-maintain housing.
  • Out-of-state inheritances. Placitas attracts retirees from across the country; their adult children typically live elsewhere.
  • Acreage owners exiting larger properties that took an MLS listing months to sell.
  • Estate sales of custom adobes with maintenance complications.
  • Pre-foreclosure in Sandoval County District Court.
  • Wildfire-risk properties with insurance challenges.

Honest note: Placitas is a premium, low-inventory market. If your property is in good shape and you have time, an MLS listing will typically fetch more. Our offers are most compelling when speed, certainty, or condition matter.
