Why Albuquerque Homeowners Sell to Us

Albuquerque is the largest city in New Mexico — about 560,000 residents, dominated by Sandia National Labs, Kirtland Air Force Base, the University of New Mexico, and Presbyterian / UNM Hospitals as anchor employers. Common seller patterns:

  • Sandia Labs / Kirtland AFB relocations. Federal labs and the Air Force move technical staff in and out on tight timelines.
  • UNM faculty and Presbyterian / UNM Hospital staff moves.
  • Estate sales of older homes in Nob Hill, Ridgecrest, North Valley, and the South Valley — often with significant deferred maintenance.
  • Pre-foreclosure situations. NM is a judicial foreclosure state — Bernalillo County District Court timeline is typically 6–9 months. We close before sheriff's sale. See our foreclosure page.
  • Out-of-state inheritances from Albuquerque retirees with adult children elsewhere.
  • Distressed properties — fire damage, foundation issues from expansive clay soils, code citations from the City of Albuquerque.
  • Tired landlords with single-family rentals near UNM or in the South Valley.
