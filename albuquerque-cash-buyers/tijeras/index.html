We Buy Houses in Tijeras, NM — For Cash.

Tijeras is the gateway to the East Mountains — a small Bernalillo County village just east of Albuquerque through Tijeras Canyon on I-40. Mountain properties, rural acreage, off-grid setups, and forest-adjacent homes. We pay cash, close in 7-30 days, and cover all closing costs.

Why Tijeras Homeowners Sell to Us

  • Off-grid and rural-acreage exits. Mountain properties that took an MLS process months to sell — we close in 30 days.
  • Wildfire-damage or wildfire-risk properties. East Mountain fires have made some properties hard to insure or finance.
  • Albuquerque-commuter relocations — many Tijeras residents work in ABQ.
  • Estate sales of mountain cabins and acreage from longtime owners.
  • Pre-foreclosure situations in Bernalillo County District Court.
  • Out-of-state owners who inherited East Mountain property and want a fast remote close.

How It Works in Tijeras

  1. Tell us about the property.
  2. We pull East Mountain comps. Mountain properties trade widely; we use comparable acreage and finish-level sold properties.
