NM Service Areas

We buy houses across central New Mexico — primarily the Albuquerque metro area and the Rio Grande corridor from Bernalillo down to Belen. We also handle East Mountain communities and rural acreage in the surrounding counties.

Cities with dedicated pages

  • Albuquerque — the full metro, all neighborhoods from Northeast Heights to South Valley
  • Rio Rancho — Sandoval County's largest city, Intel-area neighborhoods
  • Bernalillo — the town (not the county), Coronado Historic Site area
  • Corrales — semi-rural village, custom adobes, equestrian acreage
  • Placitas — Sandoval County foothills, custom homes on acreage
  • Los Lunas — Valencia County, Meta data center area
  • Belen — Valencia County BNSF rail town
  • Tijeras — East Mountains gateway, rural acreage

Why local matters in NM

NM has highly neighborhood-specific pricing. A two-bedroom adobe in the North Valley and a two-bedroom production build on the Westside — same square footage — can differ by $200k+ depending on lot, age, and condition. A flat-roof property in Corrales prices differently than the same property in Rio Rancho. Out-of-state cash buyers run algorithms that miss this; we don't.
