We Buy Houses in Bernalillo, NM — For Cash.

Bernalillo (the town, not the county — yes it's confusing) sits along the Rio Grande in Sandoval County, just north of Albuquerque. From the historic Coronado neighborhood near the Coronado Historic Site, to newer subdivisions off US-550, to acreage along the riverfront — we pay cash, close in 7-30 days, and cover all closing costs.

Why Bernalillo Homeowners Sell to Us

Bernalillo is a small town of about 9,000 — but with a distinct identity rooted in its 400+ year history and Rio Grande location. Common seller patterns:

  • Estate sales from longtime residents — many homes have been in the same family for generations.
  • Albuquerque-commuter relocations. Many Bernalillo residents work in ABQ; when jobs change they often move further.
  • Pre-foreclosure situations. NM judicial foreclosure runs through Sandoval County District Court.
  • Acreage owners who want to exit larger properties without lengthy MLS marketing.
  • Out-of-state inheritances from Bernalillo retirees.
  • Distressed properties — flood-zone concerns from Rio Grande proximity, older adobe maintenance issues.
