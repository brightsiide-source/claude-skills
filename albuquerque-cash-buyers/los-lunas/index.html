We Buy Houses in Los Lunas, NM — For Cash.

Los Lunas is a growing Valencia County village about 25 miles south of Albuquerque — and home to a major Meta (Facebook) data center campus. From the older homes near the Rio Grande to the newer master-planned subdivisions east of I-25 — we pay cash, close in 7-30 days, and cover all closing costs.

Why Los Lunas Homeowners Sell to Us

Los Lunas has been one of the fastest-growing communities in New Mexico, driven by Albuquerque overflow and the Meta data center build-out. Common seller patterns:

  • Meta data center workforce relocations. Construction phases bring waves of workers in and out.
  • Albuquerque commuter relocations. Many Los Lunas residents work in ABQ; when jobs change they often move out of state.
  • Newer-build sellers taking equity from the 2018-2022 appreciation wave in Los Lunas subdivisions.
  • Estate sales from longtime residents — older Los Lunas housing stock has significant deferred maintenance.
  • Pre-foreclosure in Valencia County District Court.
  • Out-of-state inheritances from retirees.
